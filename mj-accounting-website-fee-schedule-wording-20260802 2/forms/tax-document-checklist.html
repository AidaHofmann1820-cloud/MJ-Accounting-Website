Tax Document Checklist

MJ Accounting & Professional Services Corp

Personal Information

  • Government-issued ID
  • Social Security numbers or ITINs for taxpayer, spouse, and dependents
  • Current address and phone number
  • Bank routing and account number for direct deposit

Income Documents

  • W-2 forms
  • 1099 forms, including 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, 1099-INT, and 1099-DIV
  • Self-employment income records
  • Rental income records
  • Retirement, Social Security, or unemployment income statements

Deductions and Credits

  • Childcare expenses and provider information
  • Education expenses and Form 1098-T
  • Mortgage interest and property tax statements
  • Charitable contribution records
  • Medical expense records, if applicable

Business or Self-Employed Clients

  • Income and expense summary
  • Mileage log
  • Business bank and credit card statements
  • Receipts for major purchases
  • Estimated tax payment records
